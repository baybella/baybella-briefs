"As a Mum of [X]" — AI Avatar Compilation

Round 5 · parked bucket · four AI mums, split-screen testimonial, ~32s (~8s each)
1What We're Making
FormatSplit-screen. TOP = the AI mum holding the Baybella case. BOTTOM = real clip-on footage (AI can't do the application). Four mums, ~8s each, stitched. Engine"The first lashes that fit her life" — activation, not conversion. Re-entry into the category, not winning a comparison. PersonaAvatar B, the busy NON-BEAUTY mum. Wrote lashes off as too much faff/time/glue. Her word is "life-saver," not "money-saver." VillainHer OWN belief that lashes aren't for someone with her life. Never salon / strips / a person. RegisterWarm, inviting-back, candid — never a pitch.

The only uniform part is the shared opener "As a mum of [X]…" — each mum a different number of kids. Everything after is her own words: one concrete benefit + the real emotional pain.

2How the Lines Are Delivered (locked)

Each mum speaks her line so Sora moves her lips naturally. We then throw away Sora's audio and lip-sync a consistent Sydney voice over the top in ElevenLabs (a distinct voice per mum). So it plays as a real spoken testimonial with the lips moving — no silent captions, no AI lip-sync warp. A text overlay can carry the "As a mum of [X]" tag on screen.

4Pipeline (per mum)
1Base image — Higgsfield Soul, the prompt above. Moodboard fully OFF, character empty. Generate several, keep the most relatable/alive still.
2Lash transplant — Nano Banana Pro. Image 1 = the mum, Image 2 = the real product lash photo. Bare transplant prompt, no lash adjectives. (Nano Banana CAN put the real lashes on the AI face.)
3Animate — Higgsfield base Sora 2, 720p, aspect_ratio "9:16" (never Sora Pro — plastic). Put the spoken line in so her lips move. Lock only the framing ("steady framing"), let HER be fully alive — blinks often, small natural head/shoulder movement. NO shake/tremor language.
4Voice — ElevenLabs, one consistent Sydney voice per mum, lip-synced over Sora's thrown-away audio.
5Cull + build — run score_realism.py as the garbage-gate, then the founder's eye picks the survivors. Harvest the best 1-2 human seconds from each. Compile split-screen (AI mum top / real clip-on bottom), add the "As a mum of [X]" text tag, degrade to phone quality in CapCut.
Hard rules from the skill: never a lash/eye close-up on the AI (keep her at arm's-length selfie distance — real footage carries all lash detail). Never the word "photorealistic" in a prompt (goes plastic). Expect to cull hard — the final is the best bits of many rolls, never whole clips.
5The Cast Mix (locked)

Natural mix, not try-hard-diverse: 2 white, 1 Lebanese, 1 East Asian. Ages spread across the core band: early-40s, late-30s, mid-40s, late-40s. Locations: 1 indoors, 3 outdoors. All real-woman-her-age (not a beauty creator), wearing the lashes subtly, holding the case, candid selfie style. The lashes are the ONE constant; everything else varies.
