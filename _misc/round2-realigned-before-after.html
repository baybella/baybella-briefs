Microwave race

brief 24 · solo · silent (on-screen text + action only)
Before
Engine: effortless speed, raced against your lunch
On-screen text (hook): "seeing if I can do my lashes faster than my food heats up 🍕"
holds a snack up to camera, pops it in the microwave, sets ~30s, hits start
clips a magnetic lash on, both eyes, intercut with the ticking timer, done with time to spare

After · #4 Time Bought Back
Engine: this is the only lash-time my life takes
On-screen text (hook): "this is the only time I spend on my lashes. all month."
reheating dinner, casual, pops the food in, sets it, no rush, no race energy

Lash Health, "tension not glue"

brief 31 · to-camera · compliance: lived experience + plain why, never a medical claim
Before
Engine: it's the tension on the root, not the glue (myth-bust)
Hook
everyone blames the glue. it's actually the tension.
we all think the glue chemicals wreck our lashes. the real damage is the constant pull on the root, like traction alopecia.
magnetic sits with zero tension on your own lashes, so they get a chance to recover.
clips a pair on, both eyes
no glue, no pull, nothing gripping your lash line.
After · #3 Pressure Lifted
Engine: stop fighting your own lashes, that's one more weight off you
Hook
your lashes have been under tension this whole time. no wonder you're over it.
every strip, every set, there's a constant little pull on your lash line all day. it's one more thing quietly wearing on you.
these just sit there.
clips a pair on, both eyes
no glue, no pull, nothing gripping.
that's one less thing taking from you today.
What changed: from a correction she teaches you, to a weight lifted off you. Same tension fact, but the relief leads. Biggest stretch of the set (physical angle into an emotional engine), test it carefully.

The Glue Trap exposé

brief 32 · solo · de-vouch / exposé, anger at the old thing
Before
Engine: the glue is the business model, you're a subscription you didn't sign up for
Hook
ever notice how lash glue always runs out right when you need it?
the glue runs out, so you rebuy. it irritates your eyes, so you buy the "sensitive" one. it wrecks your natural lashes, so you book a fill to cover it.
that's not bad luck. that's the design. the glue IS the product, and you're the subscription.
magnetic doesn't need any of it.
clips a pair on, cut
After · #2 Underconsumption Flex
Engine: I bought one pair and walked out of the whole cycle
Hook
ever notice how lash glue always runs out right when you need it?
the glue runs out, you rebuy. it irritates, you buy the "sensitive" one. it wrecks your lashes, you book a fill to cover it.
that's not bad luck. that's the design. the glue IS the product, and you're the subscription.
so I bought one pair of magnetic, and I just... stopped. no glue, no rebuy, no cycle. haven't spent a cent on lashes since.
clips a pair on, cut
What changed: smallest edit. The whole exposé stays, the anger still sets it up. Only the landing changes, from "magnetic exists" to the off-the-treadmill flex of having walked out.
