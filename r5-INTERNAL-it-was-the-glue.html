2The Rule It Lives Bywhy it's not a testimonial
  • It's a collective vent, not a story. "You know that exact moment" — never "here's my before/after."
  • The switch is shown, never narrated. The VO never praises the product or announces the switch; the glue gets set down and the magnetic clipped on wordlessly, in the footage.
  • The villain is the glue, not her lashes. The reframe she keeps: it was never the lashes that failed her.
  • One relived moment only. The corner lifts → back to square one. Not a pile of complaints. The watering eye and the frustration live in the footage, never in the VO.
  • No CTA. Product named only in a soft end-frame.
3The Voiceover (locked)AI voice reads this verbatim
"Tell me I'm not the only one who finds putting lashes on the most stressful part of getting ready. You know that exact moment... you've finally got it lined up, you press it down, you think you're done — and the corner just lifts. So you're right back to square one. And you're just standing there thinking... why is this the hard part of my morning. Turns out it was never the lashes that were the problem... it was the glue, the whole time."

Voice direction: weary and real, a little exasperated, softening to quiet relief on the last line. Not a polished announcer. The "..." are real pauses — let them breathe. Unhurried pace.
