The Ten, In Full

01

"Let's settle it on camera"

Easy to applyReal-or-scam verdictLegitimacySurface

She already thinks it might be a gimmick. Don't argue - show one clean, uncut demo that answers "real or scam?" The pain: nobody wants to be the idiot who fell for a viral thing. She wants proof, not a claim. Keep it matter-of-fact; sell freedom, not "10 seconds."

Backed by ⑨ and the category's deepest doubt
Eugene's lift · time-bought-back
Without Eug"they go on in 10 seconds."
With Eug"no appointments, no fills - you're just off the whole cycle."
Speed now reads as low-status hustle; the status move is having your time back. "the ability to sit around with friends wasting time has become a status symbol." You stop selling a stopwatch and start selling the freedom she actually envies.
02

"Not one girl, everyone"

Lasts for monthsManufactured consensusLegitimacySurface

A bunch of different women all saying they're off the rebuy makes it the normal choice, not a gamble. The pain: nobody wants to go first on something unproven. A crowd already doing it is the reassurance.

Backed by ① and ⑩
Eugene's lift · underconsumption
Without Eug"loved by 50,000 women / everyone's switching."
With Eug"everyone's quietly off the rebuy."
"Everyone's buying this" is bandwagon and reads salesy now; "everyone stopped needing to rebuy" is the credible direction. "Buying less becomes rebranded as underconsumption-core. Behavior becomes identity." Same social proof, flipped from buy-more to buy-once.
03

"A pro picked the cheap one for herself"

Cost effectiveAuthority's revealed behaviourLegitimacySurface

Someone who could use anything quietly wears these when no one's paying her. Her private choice is the proof, not her recommendation. The pain: claims are cheap, who do I trust. Show what she actually wears, don't have her "recommend."

Backed by ④ and ⑤
Eugene's lift · doing-not-saying
Without Eug"as an MUA, I recommend these."
With Eug"as an MUA, here's what I actually wear myself."
A recommendation reads paid; revealed private behaviour reads true. "a decade of saying and into a decade of doing... brand as doing, not as saying." Turns an endorsement she discounts into proof she can't.
04

"I'm not selling these, I'm just done with the fight"

Easy to applyDe-vouch by inversionUpkeep / comfortSurface

Ironic regret - venting about the daily lash grind, where the relief is the whole point and nothing gets pitched. The pain: the morning routine is one more thing fighting her. The feeling: one less thing on the pile.

Backed by Biggest-Regret
Eugene's lift · pressure-lifted
Without Eug"no glue, so no irritation."

A feature she has to care about vs a pressure lifted off her. "a growing part of that narrative is what other pressures you can help me resist." Sells relief - what people buy when they're stretched.

🧪 Test
