01What We're Makingthe concept

A split-screen GRWM. Two takes of the same morning, stacked on top of each other - one take fills the top half of the screen, the other fills the bottom. In one take you do glue strips, in the other you do ours. Both start at the same second.

The point: our half finishes a whole face and walks out the door while the glue half is still on the lashes.

02The Vibehow to play it

A normal morning, done twice.

  • ☕Just get ready like you actually do.
  • ⌛On the glue side, take as long as it really takes. Don't tidy it up for the camera.
  • 👋No talking needed - your hands tell the whole story.
  • 🚪Wave goodbye like you're genuinely off out the door.
03How To Film Itsetup

Two takes. Same spot, same light, same clothes.

  • 📱Prop the phone in the exact same position both times. Mark the floor.

  • 🎬Film the glue take first, then ours. Do both properly, start to finish.
  • ✂We stack them top and bottom in the edit - you just send the two clips.

06⚠️ Key Stagingread first

The two takes have to match, or it reads as a trick.

  • 👗Same clothes, same light, same phone position both times. Mark the floor before you start.
  • 👁️Start both takes with nothing on your lashes.
  • ✂Film both sides in real time - no time-lapse. How long each one actually takes IS the proof.
  • 🏷️The strip lash must show no brand. No packaging, no logo, no label in shot.
  • 👍Use the strips properly and let them behave normally. If they go on first try, do another take - just don't fake a failure.
  • 🚫Never say the words "easy" or "effortless". There are no words at all in this one.
Agency / Post

Build notes

⏳ Casting screen - ask BEFORE the slot is assigned

The race only exists if the strip lash genuinely defeats her. A woman who is good at strip lashes kills this video, because the comparison collapses.

  • ❓"Can you put a strip lash on with glue, right now, first go? Be honest - we are looking for someone who finds them a fight."
  • ❓"When you have tried strip lashes before, what actually went wrong? How long did it take you?"

Cast 35-48. If she is quick and confident with strips, she is wrong for this brief - use her on a different row.

The engine

Persona 8, Application-Defeated. Angle cant-apply, non-switch, villain none. Ledger nerve, quoted: "you believe you physically can't put falsies on". Ledger requires she has TRIED lashes and failed, which the casting screen enforces.

What happens in it: two takes of the same morning, stacked top and bottom in one 9:16 frame. Our half finishes a full routine and walks out of frame; the glue half is still on the lashes. The wave is the setup, the empty chair is the punchline. Nothing is claimed out loud.

Why the time cost lands harder than a timer: her own routine is the clock. You measure the glue side against everything the other side finished. A number on screen would compete with what the viewer is already reading.

Where this came from - two mechanics, combined

Built 30 Jul 2026 from references/viral-mechanics-library.md. Two engines merged because they do not fight:

  • 🏁ENTRY 01b, the timed head-to-head (Kulan, 1.2M views, 13 Jul 2026). A race with an unknown outcome.
  • ✅ENTRY 01c, wrong-vs-right on one face (630K views on a 16.1K-follower account, 39× following, 28 Jul 2026). Same person, same light, nothing to take on trust.

The merge: Kulan needs two women, so the loser can always be dismissed as just slower. Running the same woman twice removes that excuse - same hands, same skill, same person, same morning.

Two founder corrections produced this version (30 Jul). First draft had her watching her own hand struggle, which is impossible - she cannot wait for herself. Second draft had both lash types going on one face at once, which is also impossible - nobody applies a glue strip and a magnetic lash simultaneously. Founder: "duh its two separate channels." Hence two takes, cut into one split frame. The fix improved the ending: the final frame is now an empty chair beside a woman still working, which is stronger than one bare eye.

Third founder correction (5 Aug): the split is HORIZONTAL - top and bottom halves of the 9:16 frame, never left/right. A left/right split gives each take a skinny vertical sliver and you only see half of each face, one eye per side. Stacking keeps each take at full width so her whole face reads in both halves.

Deliberately NOT stacked in

Per the stacking rule in the library - mechanics stack when they answer different questions, fight when they answer the same one:

  • 🚫MagLuxe 8-11s length - fights the joke. A wave-then-return needs ~20s to breathe.
  • 🚫Held comment sticker - would explain the video before it happens. The gag has to arrive unannounced.
  • 🚫GlowVane drawn diagram lines - this is a time argument, not a mechanism argument. Nothing to label.
  • 🚫Mistake-led / failure-led hooks - both want the opening slot, which the two-hands cold open already owns.

⚠️ To verify before this ships

Two risks, both real:

  • ⚠️The get-ready middle is where this gets boring. If it is only makeup steps it reads as a tutorial. It needs a wardrobe change or her leaving the room so time visibly passes.
  • ⚠️The two takes must match or the whole thing reads as a trick. Same clothes, same light, same phone position, same starting hair. This is the main post risk - check continuity before cutting, and if the takes do not match, reshoot rather than fix it in the edit.
  • ⚠️The stack crops each clip to a wide half-height panel. Her face has to sit centre-frame in BOTH takes or the crop takes her forehead or chin. Check framing on the first ten seconds she sends - and check the two takes crop to matching eyelines.

Unproven mechanic. Both source videos are organic reach on other brands' accounts - 1.2M and 630K views, no sales data behind either. Treat this row as a test, not a double-down.
