01What We’re Makingthe concept

This is the opposite of a typical lash ad. No hype, no "omg you NEED these," no excited GRWM. Think of the creators who do thoughtful cultural commentary - the ones talking about taste, about culture, about why everyone's quietly doing a certain thing. That calm, considered, culturally-aware register is the vibe. She's basically doing one of those takes - except the subject is lash extensions.

So she's not selling, she's observing. The real reason lash extensions trap you (the part no one explains), what they actually cost, and where magnetic quietly fits. She comes across as someone with taste who's genuinely thought about this thing we all do, just sharing what she sees - unhurried, a little detached, never salesy.

The goal is the "huh, I never thought about it like that" feeling people screenshot - a smart cultural take that happens to be about lashes, not a lash ad. Numbers and image cards appear as she talks; the look is chill and design-forward.

Lash extensions lock you in: the constant weight can thin your own lashes over time, so stopping looks worse than continuing, and you keep paying. Magnetic is the only option with no glue, no liner, and nothing you have to keep buying.

🎬 The Script

You’re teaching, not selling - say it naturally and hit the beats. Calm, unhurried. (Image cards + numbers get added in post - nothing for you to hold up or demo.)

“everyone thinks the problem with lash extensions is the price. the real problem is something no one explains to you.”

01 · the trap

“here's what's actually going on.”

“your natural lashes are thin, light, and they shed on their own cycle. totally normal.”

“then a tech glues an extension onto each one, and now it's carrying like two, three times the weight it's built for.”

“for about two weeks? looks unreal.”

“then your lashes shed on schedule, and you get the gaps.”

“and that extra weight, day and night, can pull your lashes out earlier than they should.”

“so you book a fill. then another. then another.”

“and every cycle, your own lashes underneath can get a little thinner.”

“so the day you finally stop, they look worse than when you started. so you don't stop.”

02 · the money

“and the money? a set's around $140. a fill every couple of weeks is another eighty to a hundred.”

“all year, you're past two grand. for a lot of people, closer to three.”

03 · what actually works

“so what actually works?”

“strip lashes? cute, but it's glue every day, and you're rebuying constantly.”

“lash serums? slow, pricey, and they can irritate sensitive eyes.”

“magnetic liner? closer, but you're painting on a liner every single day, and lining it up is a pain.”

“or the clip-on ones. two strips that clip around your own lash. no glue, no liner, nothing pulling. one kit, lasts months.”

“honestly? the only one that doesn't trap you.”
