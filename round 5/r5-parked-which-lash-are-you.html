01What We’re Makingthe concept

A “which one are you” grid video. You talk to camera and match the viewer to a Baybella lash by their vibe, with a grid of the three styles behind you (added in post).

The point: picking a lash isn’t a spec sheet, it’s a personality. One for the natural girl, one for the everyday girl, one for full glam, so one of them is hers.

Format reference: Video 1 - just for how the grid + “which one are you” structure looks. Not a wardrobe or camera guide.

02The Vibehow to play it

Playful and a little bit “I called it.” Like you’re reading your friends for filth in the group chat, but sweet. Fast, confident, a knowing smile.

Each style gets a quick, specific little character so the viewer instantly clocks which one is her. Land it light, never a sales pitch.

Delivery: fun and quick, like a personality quiz you already know the answer to. Not slow, not serious.

03How To Film Itsetup

Seated, centred, talking to camera.

  • 📱Phone on a stand, front camera, one continuous take.
  • 💡Bright and even so your lashes read clearly.
  • 🔲Leave clear space above or beside your head for the grid.
  • ✨You don’t build the grid - we add it in post. Just talk and gesture like it’s behind you.
04The Scriptwhat you say + do
Grey = what you DO. Black = what you SAY.

The script below (excluding the hook) is just a guide - keep the structure & core message, but feel free to make the wording sound natural to you.

🎬 Hook

“Tell me your vibe and I’ll tell you exactly which lashes are yours.”

🎬as you say it: straight to camera, a knowing little smile, gesture to the empty space where the grid will land
1.The natural one

“If your whole thing is ‘I just want to look like me but awake,’ you’re a Simple girl. Barely-there, no one can tell, but you look so much fresher.”

🎬as you say it: gesture up to the top-left of the grid
2.The everyday one

“If you want a bit more but still your-everyday-self, that’s Blair. The one you’d wear to work, to brunch, to the school run, and still feel put together.”

🎬as you say it: gesture to the middle of the grid
3.The glam one

“And if you walk in and you want everyone to go ‘okay who did your lashes’.. that’s Fuller. Full glam, the whole set, and people always think you got them done.”

🎬as you say it: gesture to the last quadrant, a little flourish
🎬 The reveal + close

“And here’s the bit no one expects.. they’re all magnetic, so you just clip them on yourself in like ten seconds.”

🎬as you say it: hold up your rose-gold applicator with a lash on it, place it on and let the magnets connect, then take it off cleanly so they see there’s no glue

“So.. which one are you? Because I already know 🤫”

05Key Staging - Read Firstthe one thing
⚠️ Leave grid space + show the clip-on

Leave clear empty space above or beside your head the whole time - the three-style grid goes there in post, so don’t fill the frame or block that spot with your hands.

The clip-on has to actually happen on camera. At the reveal, place one lash on and let the magnets connect, then take it off cleanly and close to the eye so people see there’s no glue. That quick on-and-off is the whole proof it’s real.

06Wear & Lightquick notes
👗 What to wear

Cute and current, something you’d film a get-ready in. Nothing too busy near your face so the lashes stay the focus.

🔆 Lighting

Bright, even front light (ring light or a big window) so all three looks and the clip-on read clearly on camera.

07Lash Visibilityread this
✨ IMPORTANT - READ THIS
When demonstrating the product, always ask yourself this:
  1. Can the camera clearly see the lashes when I use them?
Secondary questions:
  1. Are my hands blocking my face?
  2. Is the applicator covering my eyes?
  3. Is my face turned away?
