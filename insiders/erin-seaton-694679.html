❓ Your questions

Quick questions.

1. My video is live but it's not getting any ad spend. Why?

Ad spend is like views.

It works the same as organic content. On your own page, when a video performs well, the algorithm keeps showing it to more and more people.

Paid ads work the same way, so the better your video does, the more Facebook spends to show it.

If your ad isn't getting spend, that's Facebook telling us the video didn't land.

It's not a judgment on you, it just means that one wasn't the winner, and your feedback and ad metrics show you what to change for the next one.

2. How do I know how my video is doing?

Message Linda any time in the Trybe chat.

She'll send you the latest numbers on your content, including the deeper ad metrics, whenever you want them.
