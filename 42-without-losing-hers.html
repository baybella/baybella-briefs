01What We’re Makingthe concept

A stitch. The original clip is an older woman saying her lashes are too thin now to risk wearing falsies.

The point of your reply is to show that magnetic lashes are the answer to exactly that problem. They are not glued to her own lashes, so they are not pulling on them, and they are safe for someone in her position to wear.

You show it on your mum, who is the same.

02The Vibehow to play it

Helpful, not salesy. You are showing someone how it is done.

  • 💬You are answering the woman in the clip directly, like you would a friend.
  • 🧡Nobody is being corrected. Her worry is completely fair - you just have an answer for it.
  • 🏠Kitchen table, not a shoot.
⚡ Before you press record
The first 3 seconds are yours.

They decide whether anyone watches the rest. We’ve written the words - the delivery is all you, and it doesn’t need to be loud or over the top. It just needs to pull us straight into the video: if it’s a story, open like you’re mid-thought; if it’s a demo, already be doing the thing.

Skip the warm-up and land us in the moment!

03⚠️ Key Stagingread first

The removal is what makes the whole thing work.

  • ✋The video is arguing that these come off without damaging her lashes - easily, painlessly, no tugging at the few she has. The removal shot is where that either proves true or falls apart. One clean movement. If it looks fiddly or she winces, we have just shown the opposite of what we said.
  • 👀Both eyes go on, on camera. The full application has to be visible.
  • 🚫Do not feed mum the mirror line. Whatever she genuinely says is the bit we want.
  • 💬This has to feel like a video you just decided to make. The six steps are the structure - everything in between is yours. Chat, take the mickey out of each other, go off on a tangent, react to what she says. The more it sounds like the two of you actually talking, the better it works.

🚫 Word rules - get these wrong and the ad reads as a lie

NEVER claim zero contact. The two magnetic bands DO rest on her natural lashes. A woman who wears lashes knows that, so "nothing even touches them" reads as a lie and she discounts the whole video. Say "nothing’s stuck to it" and "nothing’s pulling on it" - both are in the script as written.

Say TENSION, never "the weight of them." Weakens / thins / over months, never rips out or tears out - a dramatic event she has never experienced reads as an overclaim.

Experience-framed, never medical. No growth or repair claims.
