01What We’re Makingthe concept

A stitch reaction. You reply to a girl venting that her lashes hurt her eyes, as the person who had the exact same problem and worked out why. We hand you the clip.

The point: if lashes sting, it’s the glue - not your eyes. So you use ones with no glue: a magnet on each end clips the top and bottom lash together around your own, and nothing touches your eye.

Warm, been-there, no drama. You’re not selling - you’re telling someone you get it because you lived it, then showing them the thing that actually fixed it for you. Calm and reassuring, like you’re helping out a mate who’s about to give up on lashes for good.
