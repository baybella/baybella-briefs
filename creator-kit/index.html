FAQs

1. When is my content due?

Within 7 to 14 days of your lashes arriving.

  1. Day 0Your lashes arrive
  2. Day 7Content due from here
  3. Day 14Last day to send it

Trybe also shows its own timer, which can look different. We explain it in the next question 👇

2. Trybe says my sample is overdue, but I thought I had 7 to 14 days?

You're all good, your 7 to 14 days still stands. Trybe runs its own timer and unfortunately we can't change it on our end.

  1. Day 0Sample approved
  2. Day 14Marked "overdue". Just a marker
  3. Day 180Trybe's final cutoff

Trybe's timer starts when your sample is approved, not when your lashes arrive, so it can say "overdue" before you've even had them long. It's only a marker, and your Trybe score updates the day after you post.

3. How do I send my video in?

Upload your finished video in Trybe, and send your raw clips with it.

If we think there's a stronger way to edit your video, we'll re-edit it and run that version too. You earn your 10% on every version that runs, so one video gets more than one shot.

4. When and how do I get paid?

You're paid through Trybe every week, based on the ad spend behind your videos that week.

5. How will I know if my video is being tested?

Trybe tracks it for you.

When we run your video as an ad, Trybe links that ad to your video and tracks the spend behind it, so you can see it in Trybe.

6. Do I have to post the videos on my own page?

No.

Your videos only run on our channels, so there's no posting on your own page.

7. What if my video isn't approved?

We'll tell you exactly what to change, and you can refilm it and send it back.

8. I'm stuck putting the lashes on. Where do I get help?

Start with our tutorials.

Still stuck? Message us in Trybe chat.

9. Is this program right for me?

It depends on what you want from it.

🚀 3 to 5 videos a round
