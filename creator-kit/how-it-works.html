1How you get paid

You're not paid for posting. You're paid when your ad works.

We take your video and run it as an ad on Facebook and Instagram. The better that ad sells, the more it runs, and the more you earn.

So the question is never "did I send a video?" It's "would this video make a stranger buy?"

📎 Source: Trybe on Instagram, “How creators get paid on Trybe”

2Who picks the winners

Meta decides which ads get pushed. Not us.

We can put money behind your video, but if it isn't selling, Meta stops showing it. And Meta doesn't care about views or likes. It pushes the videos that make people buy.

So what wins?
  • The lashes are on screen in the first few seconds
  • You show them going on, up close
  • You give her a reason to buy, not just something to watch

That's exactly what your script is built to do.

📎 Source: Trybe on Instagram, “How does Meta actually work?”

3Winners wear out

Every ad goes through three stages.

  • 1
    TestingA small amount of money goes behind your video to see how it does.
  • 2
    WinningA few ads take off and Meta gives them most of the budget.
  • 3
    Wearing outPeople get sick of seeing the same ad, it stops selling, and we need a new winner.
That's good news for you. There's always a spot opening up. If your first few didn't take off, the next one might take that spot. And give each new video a few days, as ads need time to find their feet.

📎 Source: Trybe on Instagram, “The 3 stages of a Meta ad” · “How does Meta actually work?”

4It's reps

Nobody goes to the gym four times and expects to be fit.

Same here. Trybe looked at their own creator data:

81%of creators who sent 4 videos or fewer made $0
90%of creators who sent 30+ videos made an income

Every video is a shot at goal. More shots, better odds. But it's not random: every shot is scripted and on purpose. That's why we write your script for you, built off the ads that are already selling.

More reps, better videos, better odds. The creators who send the most also get the most feedback back, so they get better fastest.

📎 Source: Trybe on Instagram, “Why 4 videos isn't enough” · “Post 100 times, or does quality matter more?” · “Brands would rather work with 10 creators than 500”

5Read your numbers

Stop guessing. Your numbers tell you.

Once your ad is running, three numbers tell you how it's going:

🛑 Hook rate: did they stop scrolling?

How many people watched at least the first 3 seconds.

<16%low
24%average
34%+good

👀 Hold: did they stay?

How many of those people were still watching halfway through.

<12%low
20%average
30%+good

👆 Click rate: did they tap through?

How many people who saw it tapped to the product.

<0.8%low
1.2%average
1.9%+good
Low hook rate? Change your first 3 seconds. Low hold? Get to the lashes faster. Low clicks? Give a clearer reason to buy.

📎 Source: Trybe on Instagram, “Your analytics already tell you”
