Which one's for you?

Confident putting them on while you talk?→🗣️ Talking to camera
Hard to talk and put them on at once?Film the application first, add your voice after.→🎙️ Voiceover
Really can't get them looking right on camera?→🟩 Green screen

Film more than one format. The more videos you have running, the more chances you have to land a winner.

3

🟩 Green screen

You talk while our clips play behind you, and our clips show the lashes going on.

⚡ Low-effort extraGood with the lashes?Film your talking-to-camera video first. Green screen is quick to film, so add one on top as a bonus, not a swap.
🛟 Backup optionReally can't get them looking right on camera?Green screen is your backup. Our clips show the lashes going on, so you can still make a video.

How to make one

1
Pick 2 to 4 of our clipsFrom our clip library, then save them to your phone.📂 Clip library link coming soon
2
Put yourself over the clipTikTok: tap +, then Effects, search "Green Screen", pick your clip and record.
CapCut: add the clip, add your video as an overlay, then tap Remove background.
3
Keep yourself smallSit in a bottom corner. The clip behind you is the star.
4
Edit it like any other videoHook text, tight cuts, captions. It's all in the editing guide.
