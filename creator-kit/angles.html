1. Glue gives her one shot. Magnets let her try again.

This argues that lash glue starts drying the second it hits the band, so she gets one shot to line it up before it sticks wonky, or sticks to her fingers.

Fixing it means peeling, scraping and gluing all over again. Magnetic lashes have nothing to dry. They clip to each other, so a wonky one just unclips and goes straight back on.
