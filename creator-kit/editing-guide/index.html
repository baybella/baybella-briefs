Editing guide

Send us a finished video, ready to post. Follow these steps and your edit will match the standard we run as ads.

The opening

1

Trim the start

Your hook starts on the very first frame

Cut everything before your first word. No gap, no awkward pause, no settling in. Press play and you're already talking.

2

Add your on-screen text hook

Think of it like a newspaper

  • 📰
    Your on-screen text is the headline. It tells them what the video is about."HOT TAKE: magnetic lashes 11/10, strip lashes 2/10"
  • 🗣️
    What you say is the first line of the story. It pulls them in to keep reading."I know I'm not the only girl who thought she was the worst at putting on strip lashes..."
  • ✋
    Don't just read your headline out loud. They do different jobs, so they say different things.
  • 📌
    Keep the headline on screen through the opening.
3

Put the hook in a box

Put your hook text in a box

  • White or black box, with the text wrapped inside it.
  • Get the size right: big enough that it's obvious straight away, but not so big it pulls attention off you. Notice the size in these two.
  • Above or below your face, never over it.

PSWe know not every video we share has this. It's what's working for us right now so we want it in every new video.

4

Keep it in the safe zone

Keep text away from the app's buttons

TikTok and Instagram put their own buttons and captions over the top, the bottom and the right side of your video. Anything you put there gets hidden. Keep all your text inside the green box.

The middle

5

Cut out every pause

Never leave a moment to scroll away

  • Be really picky. Cut every pause, breath and "um", and any background noise.
  • Cut as soon as you finish your words. Not before. Never cut off the end of a word.
  • Every 0.1 or 0.2 seconds counts. Tiny trims add up to a faster rhythm, and people keep watching a video that moves.
  • No empty moments. If you're not showing the product, you're talking.

The finish

6

Style your captions

Captions: TikTok Sans, white with a black outline

Use TikTok's own font for your captions, in one of these two styles.

Get the size right. Big enough to read without trying, but not so big it takes over the video. Notice the size in these two.

7

Keep the sound clean

No music, no added sound

Just your voice. No background music, trending sounds or sound effects.

8

Send your raw clips too

What we mean by raw clips: your video already cut and trimmed, just with no text, captions or hook box on it.

Send your raw clips with your edit. If we think there's a stronger way to edit your video, we'll re-edit it and run that version too. You earn your 10% on every version that runs, so one video gets more than one shot.

When you're ready to send, tick everything off on your playbook.
