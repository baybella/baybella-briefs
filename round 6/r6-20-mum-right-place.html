“You’re In The Right Place”

FormatApplication · text hook
Length~20 sec
01What We’re Makingthe concept

This is a GRWM, just the lash part. You put your lashes on with one line held on screen the whole time: "If you're a mum and you've got barely any time to yourself in the morning - you're in the right place." No talking, and you take them off at the end.

The point: lashes are not one more job. They go on in seconds.

02The Vibehow to play it

Getting ready with a friend in the room, not filming a tutorial.

  • ☕Warm and unhurried. You are just doing your face.
  • 😌Quietly pleased at the end. No big reveal.
03⚠️ Key Stagingread first

Film the whole thing start to finish.

  • 📦Open the case, put a lash on the applicator, clip it on. All of it on camera.
  • 👁️Both eyes.
  • 🤲Take them off at the end too, so we see how they come off.
  • 🔇No talking. The line on screen says everything.
  • 💄Lashes only in this one, no other makeup.
04How To Film Itsetup

Wherever you normally do your makeup.

  • 📱Phone propped up, hands free. Close enough to see the lashes clearly, not zoomed right in.
  • 💡Daylight if you can - a window is plenty.
  • 🎬One unbroken take if you can. We trim it.
  • 🔊No talking. Music goes on after.

05The Scriptwhat you do

No lines. You do not speak. This is the order things happen in.

📦 The case
🎬do: open the case and take a lash out.
🤏 Load it
🎬do: put the lash on the applicator.
👁️ First eye
🎬do: clip it on.
👁️ Second eye
🎬do: same again on the other eye.
✨ Have a look
🎬do: quick look at yourself with them on.
🤲 Take them off
🎬do: take them off with your fingers and put them back in the case.
06Lash Visibilityread this
✨ IMPORTANT - READ THIS
When demonstrating the product, always ask yourself this:
  1. Can the camera clearly see the lashes when I use them?
Secondary questions:
  1. Are my hands blocking my face?
  2. Is the applicator covering my eyes?
  3. Is my face turned away?
