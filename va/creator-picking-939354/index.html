Creator Picking SOP

Built Wed 4 Nov, 12:13am · from Arvin's calls on 99 creators, 4 Nov 2026

Your job: look at TikTok Shop creators and pick the ones worth emailing about our creator program. Each creator we pick gets a free kit of lashes, which costs us money and stock, so we only want women who will actually film good beauty videos for us. Everyone on your list already makes money on TikTok Shop and posts a lot. Your job is to judge what her videos look like.

1. The four checks

Tick a creator only if all four are true. Look at her 5 thumbnails, and click one to watch it if you're not sure.

  1. It's her, up close. The same woman, her own face, close to the camera, in at least 4 of the 5 videos.
  2. A beauty product is obvious. She's holding it up or putting it on her face (makeup, skincare, lashes, fragrance) in most of the videos. If you can't tell it's a beauty video, it's a no.
  3. She looks done up. Makeup on, hair done, camera-ready.
  4. Real variety. At least 4 different videos. If the thumbnails are the same video over and over, it's a no.
