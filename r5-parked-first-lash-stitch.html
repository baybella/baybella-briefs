01What We’re Makingthe concept

A reaction stitch. You reply to a short clip of a woman saying she keeps meaning to get back into her lashes and never finds the time. You film your part; we add her clip on the front in the edit.

The point: you don’t need to sit down and set aside time for these. No glue, no waiting, they clip on in seconds, so you do them wherever you already are.

Transcript · what she says in the clip

“...and like i keep meaning to get back into my lashes, and then it’s dropping the kids off, straight into work, and i’m always the last one i sort, it just takes a back seat, another week gone and i still haven’t-”

This is the clip you’re reacting to. It cuts off right there, and your first line picks it straight up.

02The Vibehow to play it

Warm and easy, like you’re talking to a friend - never smug. You open with “i get it,” not with a sell. The whole thing is low-key and offhand: you’re just showing how you actually fit these into a full day. Keep it real, not polished.

03How To Film Itsetup

The car is home base - start and end there.

  • 🚗Car. Front camera. Clip both lashes on while you talk.
  • ⚡Blitz - three quick spots. Pickup bench, gym change room, kitchen bench. Clip a lash on in each.
  • 🚗Back to the car. Same setup, talk to camera to finish.
