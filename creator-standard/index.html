What great looks like

This page is a set of videos we think are great, and a standard we want our content to hit.

It's not about what they're saying or how they're edited. It's about how they show up on camera. Watch for:

  • ✅Their tone — how they actually say things
  • ✅Their pacing — the speed they move at
  • ✅How they hold themselves
  • ✅Their facial expressions
  • ✅Where the camera sits
  • ✅How quickly they get going

Watch them the way you'd watch anything on your feed, and notice what keeps you watching instead of scrolling. Every one of them sounds like a real person talking to you — that's the whole thing.

Then watch your own clip back and ask the same question: would I keep watching this?
